Stryder Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (version: v1.2.0)

This policy covers the Stryder Apple Watch app and this website.

Stryder app

Stryder is a standalone Apple Watch app that times sprints for track athletes using the Watch's own sensors. This policy explains what data Stryder collects, why, and how long it's kept. Testers have additional privacy policy aspects that are described in the testers section below.

Data We Collect

Stryder collects the following data directly from your Apple Watch's sensors while you're actively using the app:

  • Location (GPS): used to measure distance covered during a sprint.
  • Motion data (accelerometer): used to detect strides.

Stryder also writes a running workout to HealthKit.

Stryder does not collect your name, email, or any account information.

How We Use Your Data

All data Stryder collects is used for computing your sprint distance and time while you're running. Location and motion data are combined on-device to calculate distance. Stryder does not currently share any data with third parties. Stryder does not transmit any data off your device, except for sprint data when you use the share sheet in the export feature.

Stryder does not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond timing and reviewing your own sprints.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Raw location samples collected during a sprint are not retained beyond that sprint session, as they are only used to compute the final distance figure.
  • For testing purposes, raw sensor data is stored locally on your device. This includes accelerometer updates, stride timestamps, and distances between GPS updates (your location is never stored)
  • Sprint results (time, distance, sensor data, and derived data from a completed sprint) are stored locally on your device and kept until you delete them in the app. The last run's sensor data after deletion will stay until you run another sprint.
  • Stryder does not currently store any of your data in the cloud. If cloud sync is added in the future, this policy will be updated, and you'll be asked for consent before any data leaves your device.

Consent & Your Choices

Stryder asks for your permission before accessing location and HealthKit data, through Apple's standard system permission prompts. You can revoke these permissions at any time in Apple Watch Settings.

If you decline location or HealthKit access, some or all of Stryder's sprint-timing features may not function, since they depend on this data to work.

Future Changes

Stryder is under active development. If a future version adds features that change how your data is collected or used, such as syncing sprint history to the cloud, this policy will be updated first, and you'll be asked for explicit consent before that change takes effect.

Stryder App - Testers

This applies to testers of the app, adding onto and overriding the general user privacy policy above.

Your name and email are shown to the developer in TestFlight. This information is only used to manage testing and will not be shared.

Crash analytics from your usage are shared with the developer by Apple.

Sensor data from your sprints that you send over will be used to improve the algorithm. Sending sprint data is always optional, and data will be deleted on request after it is no longer needed. Sprint data you send will not be shared.

Stryder website

This website has no analytics, ads, or tracking cookies, and collects no personal information. Fonts are served from this site. The host (GitHub Pages) may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's own privacy policy. Because this website doesn't track visitors, it doesn't respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your data, can be sent to: support.stryder@gmail.com
